1. The Contributor confirms that each submitted contribution is original, or

2. The Contributor grants the Project Owner a perpetual, worldwide,
   non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright licence to use, copy,
   modify, distribute, sublicense and relicense the contribution as part of
   the Project, including under the licence of the receiving repository.
3. To the extent the Contributor can grant it, the Contributor grants a
   perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable patent
   licence for patent claims necessarily infringed by the contribution alone or
   in combination with the receiving work.
4. The Contributor retains copyright in the contribution. This agreement does
   not transfer ownership, grant trademark rights, or grant any right to use
   Project names or logos.
5. The Contributor authorises the Project Owner to record the Contributor's
   GitHub account, signature date and agreement reference solely to administer
   contribution provenance. Do not include home addresses, identity documents,
   credentials or customer information in pull requests or repositories.
6. This agreement applies only after the Project Owner confirms acceptance in
   writing and applies to contributions made after that acceptance, unless a
   separate written agreement states otherwise.
