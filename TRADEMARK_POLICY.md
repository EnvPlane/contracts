# Product trademark policy (draft)

> Legal-review draft. It records intended use rules and does not assert that a
> trademark has been registered in any jurisdiction.

## Owner and scope

Oleksandr Zaiats, Germany, is the intended owner of the product word mark,
logos and associated product identifiers. Intended registration coverage is
Europe, the United States, Singapore, India and China. Use `TM` only where
appropriate until a registered-mark status is confirmed for the relevant mark,
jurisdiction and goods or services. Do not use `R` in a circle before that.

## Permitted nominative use

You may accurately refer to the software, compatible integrations and
unmodified public releases. Such use must not imply sponsorship, endorsement,
certification or affiliation by the owner.

## Forks and derivatives

Forks and derivatives may describe their relationship to the Project in plain
text, but must use a distinct product name, domain, logo and package identity.
They must not use the product mark as the primary product name, present themselves as
official, or use confusingly similar visual branding.

## Restricted use

Written permission from the owner is required for use of product names or
logos in commercial hosting, managed services, domains, social accounts,
merchandise, certification, marketing or modified product branding. The
Apache-2.0 licence of a public repository and the contributor agreement do not
grant trademark rights.

## Governance and enforcement

Trademark questions, permission requests and suspected misuse go to the
Project Owner through a private contact channel. The owner records approvals,
rejections and enforcement decisions outside public repositories. Before
filing, renewal, assignment or enforcement, obtain qualified trademark counsel
for the applicable jurisdiction and preserve evidence of actual use.
